Experience

Founded in 1992 by Ir. Urat Sitohang, PT. RAJA TUA has worked across government, state electricity, plantation, industrial and property projects since the year it opened.

His experience as Chief Quantity & Cost Control on a steam power plant project in Belawan, North Sumatra, carried out by PT Mega Eltra, is where the firm begins.
