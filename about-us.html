About the firm

Thirty years of building, engineering and finishing.

PT. RAJA TUA has worked in construction, engineering and interior since 1992, serving government, state electricity, plantation, industrial and property clients across Indonesia.

01 / Company

Company overview

Who we are

PT RAJATUA does various jobs in the field of general procurement construction as well as website and application development services.

Our company is supported by human resources who are reliable and professional in their field, so we handle work carefully and carefully and always prioritize customer satisfaction. We always prioritize service quality and product quality our customers.

02 / Our Story

Where the firm begins.

1992 → now

1992

Founded by Ir. Urat Sitohang

PT Raja Tua was founded in 1992 and was founded by Ir. Urat Sitohang. Armed with his experience as Chief Quantity & Cost Control on a steam power plant project located in Belawan, North Sumatra carried out by PT Mega Eltra, he decided and had the courage to be independent by opening the company PT Raja Tua.

1992 → now

Working across sectors

PT Raja Tua started construction projects within the government, state electricity companies, plantation companies, industrial areas, properties both state-owned and private since 1992 until now.
